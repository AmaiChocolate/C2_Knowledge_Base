# Communication Flow: Check-In to Recovery

**Last Reviewed:** 2026-08-13  
**Tags:** `#wd` `#comms` `#brevity`

## Phase 1: Check-In and Authentication

### Fighter Check-In
**Fighter**: `"TANGO, RAPTOR 11, flight of 2, Checking in"`

**WD Acknowledgment**: `"RAPTOR 11, TANGO, copy 2"`

---

### Authentication
**WD**: `"RAPTOR 11, Authenticate Alpha-Bravo"`

**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11, [coded response from authentication card]"`

**WD**: `"Good auth, standby for Lowdown"`

---

### Alibis (Equipment Status)
If equipment is degraded:

**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11, Bent radar, Link 16 up"`

**WD**: `"Copy, Bent radar, Link 16 up"`

---

## Phase 2: Lowdown and Tactical Admin

### Lowdown (Tactical Summary)
**WD**: 
```
"RAPTOR 11, Lowdown as follows:
- PICTURE, THREE GROUPS, BULLSEYE 180/60, HIGH, TRACK WEST, HOSTILE
- Active emitters: SA-10 BULLSEYE 200/80
- Weather: Scattered clouds at 15,000 feet
- Tanker TEXACO, BULLSEYE 090/30, TRACK 360
- Bingo field: NELLIS"
```

**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11, copy all"`

---

### Alpha Check
**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11, Alpha Check"`

**WD**: `"RAPTOR 11, your position BULLSEYE 045 for 20"`

**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11, good hits"` (confirms position matches)

---

### CAP Assignment
**WD**: `"RAPTOR 11, proceed CAP BULLSEYE 090/40, angels 25, maintain"`

**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11, copy CAP BULLSEYE 090/40, angels 25"`

---

## Phase 4: Commit and Engagement

### Commit
**WD**: `"RAPTOR 11, COMMIT, NORTH GROUP BULLSEYE 180/45, 35,000, TRACK WEST, HOSTILE"`

**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11"`

---

**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11, contact, BULLSEYE 180/40"`

---

## Phase 5: Tanker Coordination (If Needed)

### Fuel Check
**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11, request tanker"`

**WD**: `"RAPTOR 11, TEXACO BULLSEYE 090/30, contact AAR PRIMARY"`

**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11, switching AAR PRIMARY"`

**WD**: `"RAPTOR 11, contact me after refuel"`

---

### Return to CAP
**Fighter** (after refuel): `"TANGO, RAPTOR 11, back on station"`

**WD**: `"RAPTOR 11, copy, PICTURE CLEAN"`

---

## Phase 6: Recovery and Safe Passage

### Bingo Fuel / RTB
**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11, BINGO, request RTB"`

**WD**: `"RAPTOR 11, cleared RTB, Safe Passage BULLSEYE 090/10 to BULLSEYE 045/5, squawk 4521"`

**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11, copy Safe Passage, squawk 4521"`

---

### Handoff to Approach
**WD**: `"RAPTOR 11, contact NELLIS Approach on 327.5"`

**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11, switching, good work today"`

**WD**: `"RAPTOR 11, safe landing"`

---

## Degraded Operations (If Comms Jammed)

### Chattermark (Comm Jamming)
**WD**: `"ALL PLAYERS, CHATTERMARK, SWITCH SECONDARY"`

(All fighters switch to pre-briefed secondary frequency)

---

### Buzzer (Electronic Jamming)
**WD**: `"RAPTOR 11, BUZZER BULLSEYE 200/50"` (jammer location)

**Fighter**: `"RAPTOR 11, copy BUZZER"`
