## Overlay
Same standard NTTR E–W DCA stack as [[sc-01-single-commit]] (CAPs **EAST-L/R**, MLL **130**, fighter meld/commit **70/50**, tanker **152/80** with slide/scram **25/15**).

## Blue force
- **RAPTOR11** + **RAPTOR12**, **VIPER21** + **VIPER22** (PAIR wingmen, ≤5 nm on CAP)

## Red waves (staged ingress)
| Wave | Formation | Range | Release |
|------|-----------|-------|---------|
| FIRST | **WALL** (3 groups) | ~120 nm | T+0 |
| SECOND | **LADDER** (2 groups) | ~145 nm | T+90 s or Wave 1 destroyed |
| THIRD | **PAIR** (2 groups) | ~170 nm | T+180 s or Wave 2 destroyed |

All hostiles track east from outside MLL. Only active wave paints on scope until later waves release.

## Training goals
- Correct formation labels (WALL vs LADDER) and WIDTH/DEPTH
- Wave language (first / second / third)
- Do not overcall contacts inside the 3 NM group rule

## Debrief prompts
- Did you say WALL when the lead wave was lateral?
- Did outer wave stay outside MLL on the first call?
- When did second/third waves appear vs your picture calls?
