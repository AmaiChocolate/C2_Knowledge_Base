# ABM / MC Positional Checklist

**Last Reviewed:** 2026-08-13  
**Tags:** `#checklist` `#abm` `#c2bmo`

## Pre-mission
- [ ] JFACC/CFACC guidance, ATO/ACO intent understood
- [ ] Risk / SII / weather impacts on CAP
- [ ] Sector/lane contracts documented
- [ ] ROE declaration discipline briefed to WDs

## Execution
- [ ] Force accountability current
- [ ] Commit authority exercised/delegated per contract
- [ ] Cross-sector coordination (adjacent CRC/AWACS)
- [ ] Systems issues escalated (MSO/EPT) without losing SA narrative

## Debrief
- [ ] Crew/mission DFPs & LPs; contract validation
- [ ] Tie lessons to doctrine notes, not only anecdotes
