## Role outcomes

### MSO (Mission Systems Operator)
- Configure and maintain **BC3** scope/session basics for the crew.
- Understand **AN/TPS-75** as the radar source: range/altitude envelope, EMCON limits, EP role vs EPT.
- Execute positional checklists; recognize degraded radar effects on the **picture** the WD/ABM sees.
- Pass MSO + All Crew MQF study bar (see [[MQF_Study_Link]]).

### WD (Weapons Director)
- Build and update a doctrinal **picture** (groups, waves, labels, leading edge).
- Issue BRAA / bullseye calls; apply aspect (HOT/FLANK/BEAM/DRAG/COLD).
- Run DCA timeline: check-in → picture → commit → sort → terminate → debrief.
- Practice in [[../../simulator/README|BC3 Scope Trainer]] scenarios `sc-01`–`sc-03`.
- Drill picture labels and dimensions in [[../../picture_trainer/README|ALSSA Picture Call Trainer]] (animated formations, graded + reveal modes).

### ABM (Air Battle Manager)
- Own sector/force SA, contracts with MC/other WDs, commit authority discipline.
- Integrate ROE/ID matrix declarations with WD picture and MSO systems status.
- Lead brief/debrief loops (AF Form 4146 concepts — knowledge only until digitized).

### C2BMO (C2 Battle Management Officer / supervisory C2)
- Align CRC execution to JFACC/CFACC guidance (ATO/ACO/SPINS intent).
- Cross-check crew contracts, risk, and multi-domain integration notes ([[../01_Doctrine/ALSSA_ACC_MultiDomainOps/01_Integration_Overview|ALSSA overview]]).
- Mentor positional proficiency across MSO/WD/ABM.
