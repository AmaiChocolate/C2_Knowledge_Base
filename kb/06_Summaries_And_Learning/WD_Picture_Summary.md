# WD Picture Summary (Exam Ready)

**Last Reviewed:** 2026-11-18: checked against ALSSA ACC, ATP 3-52.4 (09 OCT 2024).  
**Tags:** `#summary` `#wd` `#picture`

## Call order
Callsign → total groups → (LEADING EDGE) label + dimensions → amplifying remarks (OPENING / CLOSING, WEIGHTED, ECHELON, TRACK) → each group: name, BULLSEYE (anchored groups), altitude, declaration, fill-ins → follow-on waves (`SECOND WAVE FIFTEEN, THREE GROUP WALL`)

## Labels
- **When**: traditional labels once COMMIT criteria are met; before that, core information
- **AZIMUTH / RANGE**: two groups, wider than deep / deeper than wide
- **WALL**: 3+ abreast, depth ≤ 5 nm
- **LADDER**: 3+ in range; total DEEP, then the SEPARATION from the LEAD GROUP to the next group
- **CHAMPAGNE / VIC / BOX**: depth > 5 nm; geometry-specific (see full note)

## Track and aspect
- All groups HOT along the threat axis: **track assumed, not said**; a common non-HOT track once after the label; otherwise per group
- Aspect bands: HOT 0–30°, FLANK 31–70°, BEAM 71–120°, DRAG 121–180°
- A group that divides: `MANEUVER SPREAD / RANGE / AZIMUTH`, pieces are ARMs (no SPLIT call)

## Fill-ins
- Altitude to the nearest thousand, never HIGH / LOW in its place; HIGH is a fill-in above 40,000 ft
- `TWO CONTACTS`; `HEAVY, THREE CONTACTS` (HEAVY alone if the count is unknown); lopsided = `WEIGHTED`

## Grouping
Contacts within **3 nm** in azimuth and range → same group

## Practice
Flash cards and the WD from Zero course (`flashcards/`), the Picture Trainer, sim scenarios `sc-01`, `sc-02`.
